Every so often, I get an idea that makes everything else temporarily uninteresting. I’ll follow it for an afternoon, or a year, just to see where it goes. Sometimes I build something. Sometimes I write. Sometimes I lose interest, but usually I find something worth keeping.

A mentor once described confidence as being able to stand calmly in front of an approaching train, trusting that you’ll find a solution before it reaches you; a feeling I know very well. I’m comfortable beginning things I don’t yet know how to do, because I trust myself to figure them out. That leaves me free to follow the questions that interest me, even when I have no idea where they lead.

I’d like to make the world a better place. I expect figuring out how will keep me occupied for a while.
