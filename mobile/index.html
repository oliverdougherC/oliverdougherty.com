Keiri

Master of Yahtzee, second to none.

Yahtzee is a game that mixes luck and strategy perfectly. It is heavily dependent on luck, but you can win more often than not by having elite foresight and strategy. How much does strategy actually help though? Assuming every single decision you made, 100% of the time, was the most statistically optimal decision, how high could your average score be? Well, that’s what Keiri does. It’s 254.59 btw. That’s the highest average score.

Lyra

Your personal tutor that won't steal all your data.

Lyra is a suite of AI-assisted study tools for students. It’s built on the foundation of data privacy and freedom. As with everything I build, there are no subscriptions, accounts, telemetry, or any other nasty stuff that has become sadly prevalent. It’s intended for users to run their own models for maximum data security, but I understand that most users (especially students) don’t have the hardware necessary for that, so users are free to choose whatever provider and model they wish.
